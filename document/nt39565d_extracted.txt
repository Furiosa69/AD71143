1. General Description 
 
The NT39565D is a dedicated 541/513/385/361 channel outputs gate driver IC for TFT-LCD 
panels. While a start pulse is triggered, output pins will sequentially output high-driving voltage 
pulses for the gate signals of the LCD panel. The NT39565D also provides shift up/down selection 
and cascade functions for dot expansion. The special pin location is designed for COF 
manufacturing. 
 
 
 
 
2. Features 
 
! 2-level Gate driver for TFT LCD panel 
! 541/513/385/361 channels output selectable for normal output waveform. 
! 540/512/384/360 channels output selectable for 2G, 2G+LCS output waveform.(Refer to chip selection description) 
! Bi-directional data shift capability 
! Digital supply voltage : 2.3V to 3.6V 
! Maximum +40V output driving voltage 
! 200KHz maximum operation frequency 
! High voltage CMOS process technology 
! COF package
